About this project

An unofficial, private, educational recreation of LMS Stanier Class 5 No. 45407 and its train on the Glenfinnan Viaduct. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by West Coast Railways, Riley & Son (E) Ltd, Network Rail, Historic Environment Scotland or the locomotive’s owners.

Accuracy (Phase 1)

This is a blockout: proportions come from published figures and from measurements of dated 2023–2025 photographs, scaled from the 6 ft driving wheels. Values still marked as estimates are listed below and in the project’s REFERENCE.md.
