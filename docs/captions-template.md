# captions.md template

Copy to `reference/captions.md`, with one block per file. File names follow REFERENCE.md §12 (L01…, C01…, T01…, V01…, D01…). Unknown fields can say "unknown".

```
## L01-right-elevation.jpg
- Subject: 45407 + tender (or coach number, or viaduct)
- Date taken: 2026-07-15
- Location: Fort William station, platform 1
- Source: own photo | URL + photographer
- Use: private comparison only
- View: right side, broadside, about 40 m away, 200 mm lens, camera about 1 m above rail
- Notes: post-2025 overhaul; tender emblem visible; Jacobite headboard carried
```

Tips:

- Elevations: shoot from far away with a long lens, square-on, on level straight track. Include the whole vehicle with a little margin.
- Dated photos matter. The loco was overhauled in 2025 and the coaches changed in 2024–26.
- For the motion (L09/L10), a short slow video while the engine moves at walking pace is ideal. Name it `L09-motion-right.mp4`.
- For human scale (C13), any photo with a person standing on the footplate or in a coach doorway helps.
