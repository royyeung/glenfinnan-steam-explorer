# REFERENCE: verified specifications

Research snapshot **2026-10-04**, updated **2026-10-05** with your decisions (§11) and photo evidence from 2025 (S33–S41). Every number below has a source and a confidence level. Anything not found is marked **UNVERIFIED** and is not to be modelled as fact. It becomes a named, flagged parameter (see PLAN §4.2) until a drawing, a photo measurement or your answer replaces it.

## 0. How to read this

**Confidence levels**

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | An authoritative primary source (owner, operator, listing authority), or two independent reliable sources that agree |
| **Medium** | One reliable secondary source (Wikipedia, or specialist model-maker research), or a value derived from High values |
| **Low** | One weak source (travel blog, simulator manual, generic database), or sources that conflict |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Not found. A placeholder is needed: it will be measured from your photos or drawings, or you will be asked |

**Units.** Imperial as published, with metric (SI) in brackets. The app works in metres.

**Snapshot caveat.** 45407 and the coach set are working vehicles, so their fittings and liveries change. The target is **"as running on the Jacobite in summer 2026"** unless you choose otherwise (Q5).

**Phase 2 status (2026-10-05):** the full Walschaerts gear is built and animated (`src/loco/motion/walschaerts.ts`).
- **From photo S33 (medium):** expansion-link centre at d 5.76 m, h 1.44 m; combination lever near d 3.45 m (low, partly hidden).
- **Estimated:** return-crank throw 0.15 m at 90° to the crank, link foot 0.40 m, die range ±0.24 m, lever proportions 0.10 / 0.70 m, crosshead arm 0.20 m, reversing-shaft position.
- **Derived link lengths** (closing exactly at mid gear): eccentric rod, radius rod, union link, lifting link.
- **Results:** valve travel 189 mm in full forward gear, 89 mm in mid gear (lap + lead), 193 mm in full back gear. Die slip ±3–34 mm. Plausible, not verified.

**Plan if no drawing arrives.** Build the linkage as an exact pinned-joint solver, so it can never disconnect. Choose link lengths so that:

1. they match photo-measured pin positions at known crank angles;
2. the valve events are plausible: valve at mid-travel plus lap and lead at dead centre.

The cutaway's info panel will say which dimensions are estimated.

## 8. Terrain and setting data

### 8.1 LiDAR coverage: important finding

On **2026-10-04** the Scottish Remote Sensing Portal's public WMS (S19) shows **no LiDAR coverage at the viaduct**. This was checked against the portal's own "Combined Data Presence" layer, every phase 1–6 DTM, the Scottish Land LiDAR Programme DTM, the 2025–26 coastal survey, and the HES project layers, with Glasgow as a positive control (`tools/lidar_coverage_probe.py`).

- **Nearest published coverage:** about **15 km east**, around Corpach and Fort William. See `docs/research/lidar-coverage-*.png`.
- **Scottish Land LiDAR Programme** (S21, S22): capture runs **May 2025 – July 2027** at 10 points/m² for national coverage. It is released in blocks about every 6 months. The first release (Jan 2026) covered Arran and other western areas; the data was revised 29 Sep 2026. Glenfinnan is **not yet published**, and its release date is unknown.
- **Attribution wording varies by dataset:**
  - Phase 5: "Crown copyright Scottish Government and Fugro (2021)" (S20).
  - Land LiDAR Programme: "Crown copyright Scottish Government © Crown copyright and database right (2026)"; the contractor is Bluesky International (S21).
  - The licence is Open Government Licence v3.0 for most datasets. Some HES datasets use the Non-Commercial Government Licence (to re-check per dataset).
  - The Fugro wording applies only if a Phase 5 tile is used, and Phase 5 does not cover Glenfinnan.

**Fallbacks** (Q3):

- **OS Terrain 50:** 50 m post spacing, 10 m contours, OGL, updated annually (S23). Attribution: "Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right <year>" (wording to re-check at download).
- **Copernicus GLO-30:** 30 m, under its own licence. Not OGL, so it needs your approval.
- **Hand-shaped near-field terrain:** the viaduct gorge, embankments and river, shaped from OS contours and your photos, and flagged as such.
